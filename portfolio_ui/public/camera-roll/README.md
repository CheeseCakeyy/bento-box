# Camera roll images

Add the selected camera roll photographs to this folder.

- Preferred formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, or `.webp`
- Recommended quantity: 8–12 images
- Recommended minimum size: 1200px on the shortest edge
- Simple filenames such as `01.jpg`, `02.jpg`, and `03.jpg` are ideal

After the images are added, the camera roll manifest can be wired to their final filenames.
